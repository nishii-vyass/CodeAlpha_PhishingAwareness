# Phishing Awareness Quiz

## Instructions

Choose the best answer for each question.

---

## Question 1

What is phishing?

A. A computer hardware failure

B. A social-engineering attack using deception

C. A backup method

D. A network cable problem

**Answer: B**

---

## Question 2

Which is a common phishing warning sign?

A. An expected message with normal context

B. An urgent request for sensitive information

C. A normal application update

D. A saved contact

**Answer: B**

---

## Question 3

What should you do with an unexpected login link?

A. Click it immediately

B. Enter your password

C. Navigate to the service using its known official website or application

D. Forward it to friends

**Answer: C**

---

## Question 4

What is smishing?

A. Phishing through SMS/text messages

B. Phishing through voice calls

C. Physical theft

D. Malware scanning

**Answer: A**

---

## Question 5

What is vishing?

A. Video phishing

B. Voice phishing

C. Virtual machine security

D. Virus removal

**Answer: B**

---

## Question 6

Does HTTPS alone prove that a website is legitimate?

A. Yes

B. No

**Answer: B**

---

## Question 7

Which is a social-engineering tactic?

A. Urgency

B. Fear

C. Authority

D. All of the above

**Answer: D**

---

## Question 8

You receive an unexpected message asking for your OTP. What should you do?

A. Share the OTP

B. Reply with your password

C. Verify the request through an independent trusted channel

D. Forward the OTP

**Answer: C**

---

## Question 9

You entered your password into a suspected phishing page. What should you do?

A. Ignore it

B. Change the password using the legitimate service and take appropriate security steps

C. Share the password with someone

D. Continue using the same password

**Answer: B**

---

## Question 10

What is the phishing safety rule used in this project?

A. Click → Trust → Share

B. Stop → Check → Verify → Report

C. Open → Reply → Pay

D. Ignore → Delete → Forget

**Answer: B**

---

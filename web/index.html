Awareness Focus

01 — UNDERSTAND

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a social-engineering attack where an attacker pretends to be a trusted person, organization or service to trick a victim into taking an unsafe action.

📧

Deceptive Messages

Attackers may send convincing emails, messages or calls.

🔗

Suspicious Links

Victims may be directed to fake websites or unexpected pages.

🔐

Information Theft

Attackers may attempt to obtain credentials or sensitive information.

02 — UNDERSTAND

How Does a Phishing Attack Work?

01

Attacker

Creates a deceptive message or website.

→
02

Message

→
03

Victim

Clicks, opens, replies or provides information.

→
04

Impact

Information, accounts or money may be at risk.

03 — TYPES

Common Types of Phishing

Phishing can appear through different communication channels.

📧

Email Phishing

Deceptive emails designed to trick users into clicking, opening attachments or providing information.

🎯

Spear Phishing

A targeted phishing attempt aimed at a specific person or organization.

📱

Smishing

Phishing performed through SMS or text messages.

📞

Vishing

Phishing performed through deceptive voice calls.

🌐

Fake Websites

Websites designed to appear trustworthy while attempting to trick users.

🧬

Clone Phishing

A deceptive copy or modification of a legitimate message.

04 — DETECT

Can You Spot a Phishing Email?

Look carefully at the following fictional training example.

URGENT: Account verification required
From: security@example.invalid

Your account requires verification today. Failure to verify may result in restricted access.

⚠ Training example — do not enter credentials.

⚠

Urgency

The message pressures the user to act quickly.

⚠

Unexpected Request

The user was not expecting an account verification request.

⚠

Suspicious Sender

The sender should be independently verified.

06 — UNDERSTAND

Social Engineering Tactics

Attackers often manipulate human emotions and decision-making.

⏱️

Urgency

"Act immediately or your account will be closed."

⚠️

Fear

Creating fear of losing an account or money.

👤

Authority

Pretending to be a trusted official or administrator.

🎁

Reward

Promising prizes, money or special benefits.

🔍

Curiosity

Using interesting or surprising messages to encourage clicks.

🤝

Trust

Impersonating someone the victim already trusts.

07 — PROTECT

How to Stay Safe

01

Stop Before Clicking

Do not immediately interact with unexpected links or attachments.

02

Verify the Sender

Check whether the message actually came from the expected source.

03

Use Independent Verification

Verify unusual requests using a trusted communication channel.

04

Protect Credentials

Never share passwords or OTPs with unexpected requesters.

05

Enable MFA

Multi-factor authentication provides an additional layer of protection.

06

Report Suspicious Messages

Report suspicious activity to the appropriate organization or security team.

08 — RESPOND

What If You Clicked a Suspicious Link?

1

Stop interacting with the page.

2

Do not enter additional information.

3

Close the suspicious page.

4

If credentials were entered, change the affected password using the legitimate service.

5

Check or enable MFA.

6

Report the incident and monitor the affected account.

09 — PRACTICE

Phishing Scenarios

These examples are fictional and created for awareness training.

SCENARIO 01

"Your account will be closed today!"

An unexpected message asks you to verify your account immediately.

⚠ Urgency ⚠ Unexpected request ⚠ Pressure to click
Safe response: Navigate to the service using its known official website or application.
SCENARIO 02

"Congratulations! You won a reward."

The message asks you to provide information to claim an unexpected reward.

⚠ Unexpected reward ⚠ Information request ⚠ Urgency
Safe response: Verify the claim through the organization's official website.
